### [Study clusters 102,684 Ash AI mental health chatbot users into five engagement phenotypes](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-78802-w) ⭐️ 6.0/10

An observational study of 102,684 users of the AI mental health chatbot Ash used k-means clustering on eight behavioral features to identify five engagement phenotypes: Early Dropouts \(52.2%\), Weekly Users \(25.3%\), Concentrated Users \(16.8%\), Intensive Users \(4.1%\), and Power Users \(1.6%\); 66.9% had at least one overnight session between 9 p.m. and 5 a.m. Among smaller subsamples assessed at baseline and three weeks, depression \(PHQ-9, n = 298; d = -0.51\), anxiety \(GAD-7, n = 298; d = -0.57\), and social support \(MSPSS, n = 194; d = 0.22\) improved. A depression-improvement gradient by engagement was replicated using model-predicted PHQ-9 in 23,813 users, with the largest improvements among Power Users \(d = -0.57\) and Intensive Users \(d = -0.50\) and the smallest among Early Dropouts \(d = -0.22\); baseline Working Alliance Inventory data were available for 11,437 users. The authors describe the evidence as correlational and pending controlled confirmation, and disclose that several co-authors are employees of the company building the model and that Slingshot AI provided funding.

rss · Nature Psychology · Nov 15, 00:00

**「Context」** Ash is a purpose-built AI mental health chatbot from Slingshot AI, which describes its work as building a foundation model for psychology rather than a general-purpose assistant. The study applies k-means clustering to eight behavioral features to derive engagement phenotypes, grouping users by how they interact with the service before comparing changes on clinical scales.

**「Impact」** The authors&\#x27; caution against treating session counts as a primary engagement metric means product and clinical teams evaluating such chatbots should distinguish usage patterns, including concentrated and overnight use, from raw session volume, while treating the reported symptom improvements as correlational until controlled studies are available.
